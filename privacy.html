Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26

This privacy policy applies to the Shomei - Japan ID Photo (証明写真アプリ) app for iOS, together with any related services operated by Anton Ryskalev (collectively, the "Application"). Anton Ryskalev is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Contact: roisy40@gmail.com

Summary

  • The Application does not collect any personal data ("Data Not Collected").
  • There are no accounts and no registration.
  • The Service Provider does not operate any servers for the Application.
  • There are no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs. The Application does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency).
  • Face detection runs on your device. Your photos are not sent anywhere.
  • Your photos, photo history and saved files stay on your device or go only where you choose to send them.

What information does the Application collect?

None. The Application does not collect, transmit or store any personal information on the Service Provider's side. The Service Provider has no servers for the Application and does not receive your photos, signature images, usage data or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

The photos you take or choose, your photo history and the files you create (print sheets, online application files and signature images) are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to the Service Provider. You can delete your photo history in the Application ("履歴" → "削除"), or remove all of this data by deleting the Application. Files you have saved to Photos or sent elsewhere stay there until you delete them.

Permissions

  • Camera: used to take your photo in the Application. Images from the camera are processed on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • Photos (add only): used only to save finished files to your photo library. The Application cannot read your photo library.
  • Choosing a photo: if you choose an existing photo, you do so in the iOS system photo picker. The Application sees only the photo you select and no other photos.

You can change the Camera and Photos permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Apple features used by the Application

Some features of the Application are provided by Apple through system frameworks. The Service Provider does not receive any data from these features. Apple's handling of data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

  • Vision: detects the face, facial landmarks and the person's outline in your photo, to check it against the requirements and to place it correctly. Processing happens entirely on your device.
  • StoreKit (in-app purchases): purchases of a single photo and the one-time "forever" purchase are processed by Apple. There is no subscription.

Purchases

All purchases are made through your Apple Account and processed by Apple. Payment, refunds and purchase restoration are handled by Apple. The Service Provider does not see your payment information.

Printing, applications and sharing

You decide where your finished files go. You can save them to Photos or send them with the iOS Share sheet, for example to the Files app, to print apps such as netprint or ネットワークプリント (Network Print), or to マイナポータル (Mynaportal). The Service Provider does not receive these files. Any service you send a file to handles it under its own privacy policy.

Third parties

Because the Application does not collect any information, no data is shared with or sold to third parties.

Email to the Service Provider

If you contact the Service Provider by email, your email address and the contents of your message are used only to reply to you. You may ask for that correspondence to be deleted by writing to roisy40@gmail.com.

Children

The Application is rated 4+ and is not specifically directed at children. Parents may use the Application to take ID photos of their children. These photos stay on the device, like all other photos in the Application. The Application does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Where required by law, the Service Provider will seek your consent to material changes before they take effect.

Previous versions of this Privacy Policy are available upon request by contacting the Service Provider at roisy40@gmail.com.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about privacy while using the Application, please contact the Service Provider by email at roisy40@gmail.com.
